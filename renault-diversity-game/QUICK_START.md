# Quick Start Guide - Renault D&I Game

Get playing in 5 minutes! This guide covers just the essentials.

## You Need

- [ ] 1 standard deck of playing cards (52 cards)
- [ ] Challenge cards (printed from Challenge Deck)
- [ ] Gold cards (printed from Gold Deck)
- [ ] Player Ability cards (printed)
- [ ] 3-6 players
- [ ] Table and 45-90 minutes

## Setup (2 minutes)

1. **Choose Difficulty:**
   - New to game? Use Challenges 1-10
   - Experienced? Use Challenges 11-20

2. **Give Each Player an Ability:**
   - Shuffle Player Ability cards
   - Deal one to each player (face up)
   - Everyone reads their ability

3. **Prepare Decks:**
   - Stack Challenge cards in order (1, 2, 3...)
   - Place Gold cards nearby
   - Shuffle the 52 playing cards

4. **Start First Challenge:**
   - Reveal Challenge 1
   - Read it aloud
   - Deal all 52 cards evenly to players

## How to Play (3 minute read)

### The Goal
Work as a team to complete as many Challenges as possible.

### Each Challenge
1. Read the Challenge card - it tells you what the team must accomplish
2. Play all your cards following trick-taking rules (below)
3. Check if you completed the Challenge's goal
4. If YES: Move to next Challenge
5. If NO: Game ends, calculate score

### Trick-Taking Rules

**A "Trick" is one round where everyone plays one card.**

1. **Starting player** plays any card from their hand
2. **Other players** must play the same suit if they have it (this is "following suit")
3. **If you can't follow suit**, play any card (but you can't win)
4. **Winner:** Whoever played the highest number of the starting suit
5. **Winner leads** the next trick

**Example:**
- Anna plays 8 of Hearts
- Ben plays 5 of Hearts (following suit, but lower)
- Clara has no Hearts, so plays 13 of Spades (high, but wrong suit)
- **Anna wins** (her 8 of Hearts beats Ben's 5, and Clara's Spade can't win)

### Communication Rules

**You CAN say:**
- "I'm strong in red cards"
- "I have three Hearts"
- "I can follow suit"

**You CANNOT say:**
- "I have the 10 of Diamonds" (specific cards)
- "Play your highest card" (tell others what to do)

**Note:** Some Challenges change communication rules!

### Player Abilities

You have a special ability (on your card). You can use it once per Challenge round. Use it wisely!

### Gold Cards

After completing Challenges 2, 4, 6, 8, 10... pause and:
1. Draw a Gold Card
2. Read it aloud
3. Discuss the D&I concept (5 minutes)
4. Continue playing

## Winning

**Score = Number of Challenges completed**

- 7-10 Challenges: Excellent!
- 4-6 Challenges: Good work
- 1-3 Challenges: Learning experience

The goal is learning, not a perfect score.

## First Challenge Walkthrough

**Challenge 1: Building the Foundation**
- **Goal:** Each player must win at least one trick
- **Rules:** Normal communication and play

**How to Approach:**
1. Deal cards evenly
2. Look at your hand (don't share specific cards)
3. Starting player plays any card
4. Everyone follows suit if possible
5. Highest card of that suit wins
6. Winner leads next trick
7. Continue until all cards are played
8. Check: Did everyone win at least one trick?

**If YES:** Celebrate! Reveal Challenge 2.
**If NO:** Discuss what happened and try again or score as "1 Challenge completed."

## Common Beginner Mistakes

**Mistake:** "I played the highest card but didn't win"
- **Fix:** You must play the same suit as the starting card. High cards of other suits don't win.

**Mistake:** "We told each other our exact cards"
- **Fix:** Can only share general info ("I'm strong in Hearts"), not specific cards.

**Mistake:** "We don't understand the Challenge"
- **Fix:** Read it together. The win condition is what you must accomplish.

**Mistake:** "This is too hard!"
- **Fix:** That's okay! The difficulty is part of the learning. Discuss what made it hard.

## Tips for Success

1. **Use abilities strategically** - Don't waste them on early, easy tricks
2. **Plan ahead** - Think about future tricks, not just the current one
3. **Communicate creatively** - Work within the communication rules
4. **Trust your team** - Sometimes you have to make risky plays
5. **Learn from failures** - Failed challenges teach you about teamwork

## After Playing: Discussion (15 minutes)

Don't skip this! The real learning happens in discussion.

### Questions to Ask Your Team:

1. **About the Game:**
   - What was hardest?
   - When did we work together best?
   - How did different abilities help?

2. **About D&I:**
   - Which challenge felt like real work?
   - What did the game teach you about inclusion?
   - Which Gold Card concept resonated most?

3. **About Action:**
   - What will you do differently at work?
   - What's one behavior you'll change?

### Personal Commitment

Each person completes:
- **One thing I'll START doing:** _______________
- **One thing I'll STOP doing:** _______________

**Be specific!** "I'll ensure quiet team members speak first in Monday meetings" is better than "I'll be more inclusive."

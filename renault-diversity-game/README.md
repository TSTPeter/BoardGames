# Renault Diversity & Inclusion: The Cooperative Challenge

A cooperative trick-taking card game designed to build awareness of workplace diversity and inclusion through experiential learning.

## Overview

This game uses cooperative gameplay to help teams explore D&I concepts including:
- Information sharing and transparency
- Perspective-taking and egocentric bias
- Complementary strengths in diverse teams
- Communication barriers and inclusion
- Microaggressions and cumulative impact
- Allyship and using privilege
- Equity vs. equality
- Psychological safety
- Intersectionality
- Systemic change

## Game Details

**Players:** 3-6 per table (scalable to large groups with multiple tables)
**Duration:** 45-90 minutes gameplay + 30-40 minutes facilitated discussion
**Total Session:** 90-120 minutes
**Type:** Cooperative trick-taking card game with asymmetrical player abilities

## Key Features

### Cooperative
Teams win or lose together, creating shared purpose and highlighting the collaborative nature of building inclusive workplaces.

### Asymmetrical
Each player has unique abilities, demonstrating the value of diverse strengths and perspectives.

### Rules-Light
Simple core mechanics with complexity emerging from challenges, keeping focus on concepts rather than complicated gameplay.

### Challenge-Based
20 progressive challenges based on real Renault D&I scenarios, from basic cooperation to complex systemic issues.

### Discussion-Integrated
Gold Cards revealed at milestones connect gameplay to D&I research and facilitate meaningful conversations.

## Learning Outcomes

By playing this game, participants will:
1. **Build Awareness:** Understand workplace diversity and the importance of inclusion
2. **Identify Behaviors:** Recognize inclusive vs. non-inclusive behaviors and cultures
3. **Apply to Context:** Relate inclusion to individual business contexts and define team-level actions
4. **Commit to Change:** Enable each player to commit to at least one personal behavioral change

## Quick Start

### What You Need
- 1 standard deck of playing cards (52 cards)
- Challenge Deck (printed)
- Gold Deck (printed)
- Player Ability cards (printed)
- 3-6 players
- 90-120 minutes

### Setup (5 minutes)
1. Shuffle and deal one Player Ability card to each player
2. Place Challenge Deck and Gold Deck nearby
3. Reveal first Challenge card
4. Shuffle playing cards and deal evenly to all players

### How to Play
1. Read the Challenge card's win condition
2. Play cards in tricks (follow suit, high card wins)
3. Check if Challenge is complete
4. If successful, advance to next Challenge
5. Every 2 Challenges, reveal and discuss a Gold Card
6. Continue until team fails a Challenge
7. Debrief and discuss applications to real work

See [Game Rules](rules/GAME_RULES.md) for complete instructions.

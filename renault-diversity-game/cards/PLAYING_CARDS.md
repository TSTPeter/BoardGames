# Playing Cards - Renault D&I Game

The game uses a standard 52-card deck with four suits representing different dimensions of diversity and inclusion. While suits don't impact game mechanics (you still follow suit and play high to win), they provide rich resources for facilitated discussion.

## The Four Suits

### Hearts (Red) - Representation & Hiring
**Symbolizes:** Getting diverse talent through the door

**Key Themes:**
- Recruitment strategies
- Hiring bias
- Diverse candidate pipelines
- Interview processes
- Job description accessibility
- Proactive outreach

**Discussion Connections:**
- Who gets hired at Renault?
- Where do we recruit?
- What barriers exist in our hiring process?
- How do we assess "culture fit" vs. "culture add"?

**Example Card Meanings (for facilitated discussion):**
- **Ace of Hearts:** First step - acknowledging the need for diverse hiring
- **King of Hearts:** Senior leadership commitment to representation
- **Queen of Hearts:** Mid-level managers who champion diverse hiring
- **Jack of Hearts:** Recruiters and HR partners
- **10 of Hearts:** Strong diverse candidate pipeline
- **5 of Hearts:** Moderate diversity in applicant pool
- **2 of Hearts:** Token diversity efforts

---

### Diamonds (Red) - Career Development & Promotion
**Symbolizes:** Ensuring everyone can advance and grow

**Key Themes:**
- Promotion equity
- Sponsorship and mentorship
- Career path transparency
- Leadership development
- Performance evaluation fairness
- Access to high-visibility projects

**Discussion Connections:**
- Who gets promoted at Renault?
- What informal networks enable advancement?
- How do we identify "high potential"?
- Are career paths equally clear to everyone?

**Example Card Meanings (for facilitated discussion):**
- **Ace of Diamonds:** Breaking through to senior leadership
- **King of Diamonds:** Executive sponsorship
- **Queen of Diamonds:** Mentor who actively develops others
- **Jack of Diamonds:** High-visibility project assignment
- **10 of Diamonds:** Clear career path and development plan
- **5 of Diamonds:** Some growth opportunities
- **2 of Diamonds:** Stagnant career trajectory

---

### Clubs (Black) - Workplace Culture & Belonging
**Symbolizes:** Creating an environment where everyone feels they belong

**Key Themes:**
- Psychological safety
- Inclusive culture
- Microaggressions
- Social belonging
- Work-life integration
- Cultural norms and expectations
- Informal networks

**Discussion Connections:**
- Who feels like they "fit" at Renault?

- When do people feel they can be authentic?
- How do we respond to cultural differences?

**Example Card Meanings (for facilitated discussion):**

- **King of Clubs:** Leaders who model inclusive behavior
- **Queen of Clubs:** Team members who create belonging
- **Jack of Clubs:** Safe space to be authentic

- **5 of Clubs:** Partial belonging - some "in," some "out"
- **2 of Clubs:** Exclusionary culture

## Using Suits in Facilitated Discussion

### During Gameplay

While playing, suits follow standard trick-taking rules:
- Must follow the suit led
- Highest card of led suit wins
- Suits are equal in power (no trump suit by default)

### During Debrief

After gameplay, suits become discussion prompts:

**Suit Distribution Analysis:**
"Look at the tricks you won. Which suits did you win most often?"
- Heavy in Hearts: You had access to representation/hiring resources
- Heavy in Diamonds: You had career advancement opportunities
- Heavy in Clubs: You experienced belonging
- Heavy in Spades: You had voice and influence

**Connection to Real Experience:**
"Think about your actual experience at Renault. Which 'suit' do you feel you have most/least of?"
- Hearts: Do you see diverse hiring around you?
- Diamonds: Do you have clear career growth opportunities?
- Clubs: Do you feel you belong and can be authentic?
- Spades: Is your voice heard and valued?

### Specific Discussion Prompts by Suit

**Hearts - Representation & Hiring:**
1. "What barriers exist for diverse candidates in our recruitment?"
2. "Where could we expand our talent pipelines?"
3. "How do we define 'qualified' in ways that might exclude?"

**Diamonds - Career Development & Promotion:**
1. "Who gets access to career-accelerating opportunities?"
2. "What informal networks drive advancement?"
3. "How transparent are our promotion criteria?"

**Clubs - Workplace Culture & Belonging:**
1. "Who do our cultural norms favor?"
2. "When do people feel they need to 'cover' or hide parts of themselves?"
3. "What small behaviors signal 'you belong' or 'you don't'?"

**Spades - Voice & Participation:**
1. "Whose ideas typically get adopted?"
2. "When do people self-censor rather than speak up?"
3. "How do we respond to voices that challenge the status quo?"

## Facilitation Activities Using Cards

### Activity 1: Personal Reflection
**After gameplay, during debrief:**

1. Each player looks at the cards they won
2. Choose one card that represents your current D&I experience at Renault
3. Share: "I chose [card] because..."
4. Team discusses patterns and differences

**Example:**
"I chose the 3 of Spades because I feel my voice is heard sometimes but often overlooked, especially in senior leadership meetings."

---

### Activity 2: Aspiration Mapping
**During final discussion:**

1. Each player selects a card representing where they want Renault to be
2. Place cards in the center
3. Discuss: "What would it take to get from where we are to where we want to be?"
4. Identify concrete actions

**Example:**
"I want us to reach the King of Clubs - a culture where everyone truly belongs. To get there, we need to..."

---

### Activity 3: Suit Audit
**For ongoing team reflection:**

1. Team rates their current state in each suit (1-13)
2. Plot on a visual diagram (like a compass with four directions)
3. Identify which "suit" needs most attention
4. Create action plan for that dimension

**Example:**
- Hearts (Hiring): 8/13 - Good diverse recruitment
- Diamonds (Promotion): 4/13 - Need work on equitable advancement
- Clubs (Belonging): 6/13 - Some inclusion, some exclusion
- Spades (Voice): 5/13 - Many voices still unheard

Focus area: Diamonds - Career Development

---

### Activity 4: The Missing Suits
**Advanced reflection:**

"In the game, you needed all four suits to succeed. In your real work:"
1. "Which suit is most abundant in your experience?"
2. "Which suit is missing or scarce?"
3. "How does the absence of one suit affect the others?"

**Insight:**
You can't build sustainable inclusion by focusing on just one dimension. Need all four:
- Hire diverse talent (Hearts)
- Develop and promote equitably (Diamonds)
- Create belonging (Clubs)
- Ensure voice and power (Spades)

## Color Symbolism

### Red Suits (Hearts & Diamonds)
**Positive, Active, Growth-Oriented**
- Hearts (Red): Bringing people IN (hiring, representation)
- Diamonds (Red): Moving people UP (development, promotion)

### Black Suits (Clubs & Spades)
**Foundation, Culture, Empowerment**
- Clubs (Black): Sense of BELONGING (culture, inclusion)
- Spades (Black): Having VOICE (participation, power)

### Discussion Note:
"Notice that you need both red suits (active talent moves) AND black suits (cultural foundation) to win. You can't succeed with hiring alone - you need belonging. You can't develop people without giving them voice."

## Advanced Variant: Trump Suits

For expert play, challenges can designate a "trump suit":

**Trump Suit Rules:**
- One suit is designated as "trump" for a challenge
- Trump cards beat all other suits, regardless of number
- Still must follow suit if possible
- If cannot follow suit, may play trump
- Adds strategic complexity

**Thematic Use:**
- Hearts as Trump: "This challenge prioritizes hiring diverse talent"
- Diamonds as Trump: "This challenge prioritizes equitable promotion"
- Clubs as Trump: "This challenge prioritizes culture and belonging"
- Spades as Trump: "This challenge prioritizes voice and participation"

**Example:**
Challenge: "Address the leadership diversity gap"
Trump Suit: Diamonds (Career Development)
Meaning: Promotion and development are most critical for this issue

## Learning Objectives: Suits as D&I Dimensions

By using themed suits, players will:

1. **Recognize Multiple Dimensions:** D&I isn't monolithic - it has distinct aspects
2. **Understand Interconnection:** All dimensions work together (like needing all suits in game)
3. **Identify Gaps:** Some dimensions may be strong, others weak in their organization
4. **Personalize Learning:** Connect abstract concepts to concrete experiences
5. **Create Shared Language:** Team develops common vocabulary for D&I discussion

**Most Importantly:** The suits provide a framework for ongoing team reflection, not just one-time training. Teams can return to the "four suits" model in regular work.

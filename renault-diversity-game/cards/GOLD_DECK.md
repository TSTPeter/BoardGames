# Gold Deck - Renault D&I Game

Gold Cards are revealed at milestone moments during gameplay. Each card connects game mechanics to a specific D&I concept, providing the framework for facilitated discussion and learning.

## When to Reveal Gold Cards

- After completing Challenge 2
- After completing Challenge 4
- After completing Challenge 6
- After completing Challenge 8
- After completing Challenge 10
- Continue every 2 challenges for advanced play

## How to Use Gold Cards

1. **Pause Gameplay:** Stop between challenges
2. **Read Aloud:** Facilitator reads the entire Gold Card to the team
3. **Reflect:** Team discusses the connection between the game and concept (3-5 minutes)
4. **Share:** Each player shares one example from their own experience
5. **Commit:** Encourage one actionable takeaway
6. **Resume:** Continue to next challenge

## GOLD CARD 1: The Power of Information Sharing

**Revealed After:** Challenge 2

### D&I Concept: Transparency and Information Equity

**Game Connection:**
In this game, you cannot directly tell teammates what cards you have. You must work within communication constraints to succeed. Similarly, in the workplace, important information doesn't always reach everyone equally.

**Key Insights:**
- **Information Asymmetry:** Some people have more information than others, creating power imbalances
- **Active Sharing:** Inclusive teams actively share context, not just when asked
- **Creative Communication:** When formal channels fail, inclusive colleagues find alternative ways to share knowledge

### Discussion Questions
1. In your team, who typically has access to important information first?
2. What information might you assume "everyone knows" but actually isn't widely shared?
3. How can you more actively share context with colleagues who might be left out of informal networks?

### Real Renault Application
- Share meeting notes with remote team members
- CC broader distribution on important decisions
- Create documentation in multiple languages
- Ensure new hires get context, not just tasks

## GOLD CARD 2: Egocentric Bias and Perspective-Taking

**Revealed After:** Challenge 4

### D&I Concept: Understanding Different Perspectives
*Reference: Ross & Sicoly (1979) - Egocentric Biases in Availability and Attribution*

**Game Connection:**
You're trying to understand what cards your teammates hold based on limited information. You must imagine their perspective, not just focus on your own hand. This is perspective-taking.

**Key Insights:**
- **Egocentric Bias:** We overestimate how much others know about our situation
- **False Consensus:** We assume others see things as we do
- **Active Perspective-Taking:** Inclusion requires deliberately considering others' viewpoints

### Discussion Questions
1. When have you assumed others understood your constraints or challenges without you explaining?
2. How might your experience at Renault differ from someone with a different background?
3. What questions could you ask to better understand a colleague's perspective?

### Real Renault Application
- Before making assumptions, ask questions
- Consider constraints colleagues face that you don't
- Recognize that "obvious" solutions may not work for everyone
- Listen to understand, not to respond

### Personal Commitment
"I will practice perspective-taking by..."

## GOLD CARD 3: Asymmetrical Contributions and Complementary Strengths

**Revealed After:** Challenge 6

### D&I Concept: Valuing Different Contributions

**Game Connection:**
Each player has different abilities and different cards. No one can win alone. Success requires recognizing that different doesn't mean better or worse—it means complementary.

**Key Insights:**
- **Complementary Strengths:** Diverse teams succeed because people bring different capabilities
- **Equal Value, Different Roles:** Not everyone contributes the same way; all contributions matter
- **Synergy:** The team's capability exceeds any individual's

### Discussion Questions
1. What unique strengths do different members of your team bring?
2. When do we mistakenly value certain types of contributions over others?
3. How can you better recognize contributions that aren't like yours?

### Real Renault Application
- Value different working styles (detail-oriented vs. big-picture)
- Recognize both vocal and quiet contributors
- Appreciate technical expertise AND interpersonal skills
- Celebrate diverse problem-solving approaches

## GOLD CARD 4: Communication Barriers and Exclusion

**Revealed After:** Challenge 8

### D&I Concept: Inclusive Communication

**Game Connection:**
When communication was restricted (Challenge 6), the game became much harder. In workplaces, communication barriers—language, jargon, cultural norms—exclude people unintentionally.

**Key Insights:**
- **Invisible Barriers:** Communication challenges aren't always obvious to those unaffected
- **Extra Effort:** People facing barriers must work harder to participate
- **Adaptation Required:** Inclusive teams adapt their communication style

### Discussion Questions
1. What communication norms in your team might exclude someone?
2. When have you felt excluded because of how communication happened?
3. What jargon or acronyms do we use that might confuse newcomers or non-native speakers?

### Real Renault Application
- Define acronyms and technical terms
- Share written summaries of verbal discussions
- Speak clearly in multilingual settings
- Use visual aids, not just verbal explanation
- Check understanding, don't assume it

### Personal Commitment
"I will communicate more inclusively by..."

## GOLD CARD 5: The Compound Effect of Exclusion

**Revealed After:** Challenge 10

### D&I Concept: Microaggressions and Cumulative Impact

**Game Connection:**
In Challenge 13, each time someone couldn't follow suit, they lost additional cards. Small exclusions compound, making future participation even harder. This is how microaggressions work.

**Key Insights:**
- **Cumulative Impact:** Small slights accumulate over time
- **Downward Spiral:** Each exclusion makes the next more likely
- **Invisible to Others:** Those unaffected may not notice the pattern
- **Intervention Needed:** Active inclusion prevents the spiral

### Discussion Questions
1. What small behaviors might make someone feel "left out" repeatedly?
2. How do we notice when someone is gradually disengaging?
3. What can we do when we observe this pattern affecting a colleague?

### Real Renault Application
- Notice who gets interrupted in meetings
- Track who gets invited to informal gatherings
- Monitor who gets credit for ideas
- Address patterns, not just individual incidents
- Check in with colleagues who seem quiet lately

### Personal Commitment
"I will notice and interrupt exclusion by..."

## GOLD CARD 6: Active Allyship and Using Privilege

**Revealed After:** Challenge 12 (Advanced Mode)

### D&I Concept: Allyship in Action

**Game Connection:**
In Challenge 16, the player with the most power (high cards) had to actively help others succeed. True allyship means those with advantage use it to support others.

**Key Insights:**
- **Strategic Restraint:** Sometimes helping means stepping back
- **Active Support:** Privilege should be used to enable others
- **Intentional Redistribution:** Those with more must deliberately share

### Discussion Questions
1. What advantages or privileges do you have in your workplace?
2. How could you use your position to support colleagues with less privilege?
3. What does "stepping back" look like in practice?

### Real Renault Application
- Amplify others' ideas in meetings
- Recommend colleagues for opportunities
- Share access to networks and decision-makers
- Decline opportunities to create space for others
- Speak up when someone is interrupted or ignored

## GOLD CARD 7: Equity vs. Equality

**Revealed After:** Challenge 14 (Advanced Mode)

### D&I Concept: Equitable Support

**Game Connection:**
In Challenge 15 (Pay Equity), the team had to give more support to the player starting with less. Equity means giving people what they need to succeed, which isn't always the same for everyone.

**Key Insights:**
- **Equality = Same Treatment:** Everyone gets the same resources
- **Equity = Fair Treatment:** People get resources based on their needs
- **Different Starting Points:** Not everyone begins with the same advantages
- **Systemic Support:** Organizations must actively create equity

### Discussion Questions
1. Where do you see gaps in starting position at Renault?
2. What support might someone need that others don't?
3. How can we provide equitable (not just equal) opportunities?

### Real Renault Application
- Flexible working for caregivers
- Language support for non-native speakers
- Mentorship for underrepresented groups
- Additional ramp-up time for career changers
- Accommodations for disabilities

### Personal Commitment
"I will support equity by..."

## GOLD CARD 8: Psychological Safety and Risk-Taking

**Revealed After:** Challenge 16 (Advanced Mode)

### D&I Concept: Creating Safe Environments
*Reference: Amy Edmondson's research on psychological safety*

**Game Connection:**
In Challenge 19, you had to deliberately lose tricks you could have won. This requires trust that the team won't punish your risk-taking. That's psychological safety.

**Key Insights:**
- **Safety to Fail:** People can take interpersonal risks without fear
- **Learning from Mistakes:** Failures are learning opportunities, not punishments
- **Speaking Up:** People share ideas, questions, and concerns freely
- **Foundation for Inclusion:** Without safety, diverse voices stay silent

### Discussion Questions
1. Do team members feel safe admitting mistakes in your team?
2. What happens when someone proposes an idea that fails?
3. How do we respond when someone asks a "basic" question?

### Real Renault Application
- Thank people for raising concerns
- Share your own mistakes and learning
- Never punish someone for speaking up
- Encourage questions, especially "obvious" ones
- Frame failures as experiments

### Personal Commitment
"I will build psychological safety by..."

## GOLD CARD 9: Intersectionality and Complex Identity

**Revealed After:** Challenge 18 (Advanced Mode)

### D&I Concept: Multiple, Intersecting Identities
*Reference: Kimberlé Crenshaw's intersectionality framework*

**Game Connection:**
In Challenge 12, winning cards had to differ in BOTH suit AND odd/even. People aren't just one thing—they have multiple, intersecting identities that create unique experiences.

**Key Insights:**
- **Multiple Identities:** Everyone has many identity dimensions (race, gender, age, etc.)
- **Intersecting Experiences:** These dimensions interact in complex ways
- **Unique Perspectives:** No two people have identical experiences
- **Avoid Single-Category Thinking:** Can't reduce people to one characteristic

### Discussion Questions
1. What identity dimensions are most salient in your workplace?
2. How might someone's experience differ based on multiple intersecting identities?
3. When do we oversimplify by focusing on just one dimension of diversity?

### Real Renault Application
- Recognize that "women" is not a monolithic group
- Consider how age, culture, and role intersect
- Avoid assumptions based on single characteristics
- Listen to individual experiences, not just demographic categories
- Design initiatives that address multiple dimensions

"I will recognize intersectionality by..."

**Revealed After:** Challenge 20 (Advanced Mode)

### D&I Concept: Organizational Transformation

**Game Connection:**
Challenge 20 required addressing multiple constraints simultaneously. Real systemic change isn't about one initiative—it's about transforming multiple interconnected systems.

**Key Insights:**
- **Interconnected Systems:** Recruitment, development, culture, and processes all affect D&I
- **Long-term Commitment:** Change requires sustained effort, not one-time programs
- **Everyone's Responsibility:** Systemic change requires action at all levels
- **Progress, Not Perfection:** Improvement is iterative

### Discussion Questions
1. What systems at Renault impact diversity and inclusion?
2. Where have you seen genuine progress? What enabled it?
3. What's one system you can influence in your role?

### Real Renault Application
- Advocate for inclusive hiring processes
- Support policy changes (flexibility, leave, etc.)
- Model inclusive behavior consistently
- Hold leadership accountable
- Celebrate progress while continuing to push forward

## Facilitator Guide for Gold Cards

### Timing
- Don't rush: Allow 5-10 minutes per Gold Card
- Balance discussion with gameplay momentum
- Save deeper discussion for final debrief

### Discussion Facilitation
- Ensure everyone speaks, not just the vocal players
- Connect back to specific game moments
- Ask for concrete examples, not just abstract agreement
- Encourage vulnerability and authentic sharing

### Adapting to Your Team
- Modify discussion questions based on team's experience
- Use specific Renault examples you know
- Adjust depth based on team's engagement level
- Be prepared for emotional responses

### Academic References
- Ross & Sicoly (1979): Egocentric biases in availability and attribution
- Amy Edmondson: Psychological safety research
- Kimberlé Crenshaw: Intersectionality theory
- Additional references available in facilitator's full guide

# Player Ability Cards - Renault D&I Game

Each player receives one unique ability card. These abilities represent different strengths and perspectives that diverse team members bring. Abilities are designed to be game-focused (not role-playing) while still illustrating D&I concepts.

## Design Principles

- **Asymmetrical:** No two players play the same way
- **Balanced:** All abilities are useful; none is strictly "better"
- **Strategic:** Abilities require thoughtful timing and coordination
- **Thematic:** Each represents a real workplace strength

---

## ABILITY 1: The Connector

**Symbolic Of:** Relationship builders, network weavers, social cohesion

**Ability Name:** Bridge Builder

**Power:**
Once per round, after any trick is complete, you may pass one card from your hand to another player, and they must pass one card back to you.

**Rules:**
- Both players choose which card to exchange
- Exchange happens between tricks, not during a trick
- Announce "Building a Bridge" when using
- Cannot be used during the final trick
- Single use per round

**Strategic Value:**
- Helps redistribute cards where they're needed most
- Enables coordination between players
- Can rescue a struggling teammate
- Requires trust about what's needed

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who connect others, share resources, and build collaboration across boundaries. In workplaces, connectors help resources and information flow to where they're needed most.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"Who in your team acts as a connector? How do they create value through relationships?"

---

## ABILITY 2: The Observer

**Symbolic Of:** Active listeners, data analysts, pattern recognizers

**Ability Name:** Deep Insight

**Power:**
Once per round, you may ask any other player one yes/no question about their hand, and they must answer truthfully.

**Rules:**
- Can be used at any time, even during communication restrictions
- Question must be answerable with yes/no
- Player must answer truthfully
- Examples: "Do you have any Hearts?" "Is your highest card above 10?"
- Single use per round

**Strategic Value:**
- Gathers critical information
- Helps team plan strategy
- Can overcome communication barriers
- Requires asking the right question

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who ask good questions, listen carefully, and notice patterns others miss. In workplaces, observers provide crucial insights by paying attention to what's unsaid.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"When has asking the right question unlocked a solution for your team?"

---

## ABILITY 3: The Advocate

**Symbolic Of:** Allies, sponsors, those who amplify others' voices

**Ability Name:** Amplify

**Power:**
Once per round, you may play a card on behalf of another player during their turn (with their permission). The card counts as if they played it.

**Rules:**
- You play the card from their hand, not yours
- They choose which card you play for them
- This counts as their play for that trick
- Announce "I'm Advocating" when using
- Requires their consent
- Single use per round

**Strategic Value:**
- Helps players who are stuck or uncertain
- Can prevent a mistake
- Demonstrates trust and support
- Useful when player has communication restrictions

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who use their position to support others, speak up on their behalf, and share power. In workplaces, advocates help ensure everyone's voice is heard.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"When have you advocated for someone whose voice wasn't being heard?"

---

## ABILITY 4: The Innovator

**Symbolic Of:** Creative thinkers, rule-questioners, those who see new possibilities

**Ability Name:** Reframe

**Power:**
Once per round, you may declare that the LOWEST card of the led suit wins the trick instead of the highest.

**Rules:**
- Must be declared before any player plays to the trick
- Affects only that single trick
- All other rules still apply (must follow suit, etc.)
- Announce "Reframing" when using
- Cannot be used on final trick
- Single use per round

**Strategic Value:**
- Turns weakness into strength
- Enables unexpected wins
- Requires advance planning
- Can completely change strategy

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who challenge assumptions, think differently, and see opportunities others miss. In workplaces, innovators help teams break out of conventional thinking.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"When has an unconventional approach solved a problem traditional methods couldn't?"

---

## ABILITY 5: The Mentor

**Symbolic Of:** Experienced guides, knowledge sharers, patient teachers

**Ability Name:** Share Wisdom

**Power:**
Once per round, before a trick begins, you may reveal up to three cards from your hand to all players.

**Rules:**
- Choose which cards to reveal
- All players see them
- Cards remain in your hand
- Happens before the trick begins
- Provides information to help team planning
- Single use per round

**Strategic Value:**
- Reduces uncertainty for team
- Helps others plan their plays
- Demonstrates transparency
- Can coordinate complex tricks

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who share their knowledge and experience openly, helping others succeed. In workplaces, mentors accelerate others' growth through transparency and guidance.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"Who has mentored you? How did they share their knowledge?"

---

## ABILITY 6: The Adapter

**Symbolic Of:** Flexible thinkers, cultural translators, resilient problem-solvers

**Ability Name:** Flexibility

**Power:**
Twice per round, after seeing the card led, you may swap one card from your hand with the top card of the deck (unseen).

**Rules:**
- Look at the led card first
- Discard one card from your hand to bottom of deck
- Draw the top card of the deck
- You must play immediately (either the new card or another from your hand)
- Can be used twice per round
- Announce "Adapting" when using

**Strategic Value:**
- Provides second chances
- Helps when you're stuck
- Risk/reward (new card is unknown)
- Increases flexibility

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who adapt to changing circumstances, work across contexts, and remain effective in ambiguity. In workplaces, adapters bridge cultural differences and navigate change.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"When have you had to adapt your approach to work effectively in a new context?"

---

## ABILITY 7: The Coordinator (for 7+ players)

**Symbolic Of:** Project managers, organizers, those who align efforts

**Ability Name:** Orchestrate

**Power:**
Once per round, before any cards are played, you may specify the exact order in which players will play to the next trick (instead of clockwise).

**Rules:**
- Choose the sequence for one complete trick
- All players must play in your specified order
- First player still leads the suit
- Standard trick-taking rules apply
- Announce "Orchestrating" when using
- Single use per round

**Strategic Value:**
- Optimizes play sequence
- Enables complex coordination
- Helps ensure right person wins
- Requires understanding everyone's position

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who organize collective action, align diverse efforts toward common goals, and ensure everyone's contribution happens at the right time. In workplaces, coordinators enable diverse teams to work in sync.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"How does your team ensure everyone's contributions are coordinated effectively?"

---

## ABILITY 8: The Champion (for 8 players)

**Symbolic Of:** Bold risk-takers, those who challenge the status quo

**Ability Name:** Bold Move

**Power:**
Once per round, you may win a trick even if you didn't play the highest card of the led suit.

**Rules:**
- Declare "Bold Move" after all cards are played but before winner is determined
- You win that trick regardless of normal rules
- Can only be used if you followed suit
- Very powerful but single use
- Use strategically for critical tricks

**Strategic Value:**
- Guarantees winning a crucial trick
- Provides insurance for high-stakes moments
- Can save the team from failure
- Must be used wisely (only once)

**D&I Connection:**
Represents people who take bold stands, speak truth to power, and push for change even when it's risky. In workplaces, champions drive transformation by having the courage to act.

**Discussion Prompt:**
"When have you or a colleague taken a bold stance for an important principle?"

## Ability Selection and Setup

### Random Assignment (Recommended)
1. Shuffle all ability cards
2. Deal one to each player face-up
3. Each player reads and understands their ability
4. Players briefly discuss how abilities might work together

**Benefit:** Emphasizes that we don't choose our "diversity" - we work with what we bring

### Strategic Selection
1. Place all ability cards face-up
2. Players draft in turn (or discuss and choose together)
3. Each player selects an ability that resonates with them

**Benefit:** Players connect personally with their ability's meaning

### Rotating Abilities
In repeat play:
- Players swap abilities between games
- Experience different perspectives and strengths
- Deepens understanding of complementary contributions

**Benefit:** Everyone experiences being "good at different things"

---

## Balancing Notes for Facilitators

### Power Level
All abilities are designed to be:
- **Useful in most challenges** - no ability is irrelevant
- **Situationally powerful** - each shines in different moments
- **Requiring skill** - effectiveness depends on wise use
- **Promoting cooperation** - all abilities help the team, not just the individual

### Player Count Adjustments
- **3 players:** Use Abilities 1-3
- **4 players:** Use Abilities 1-4
- **5 players:** Use Abilities 1-5
- **6 players:** Use Abilities 1-6
- **7 players:** Add Ability 7
- **8 players:** Add Ability 8

### Advanced Variant: Dual Abilities
For expert players:
- Each player gets TWO abilities
- Can use each once per round
- Significantly increases complexity
- Represents how everyone has multiple strengths

## Discussion Guide: Abilities and Real Strengths

### Facilitation Questions

**After Setup:**
1. "How does your ability make you play differently than others?"
2. "Whose ability complements yours? How might you work together?"
3. "Which ability seems most powerful? Why?"

**During Play:**
- Notice when abilities enable success
- Point out moments of complementary strengths
- Highlight creative ability combinations

**After Game:**
1. "Which ability proved most valuable? In what situations?"
2. "How did having different abilities make the team stronger?"
3. "If you could choose your ability, which would you pick? Why?"
4. "How does this relate to diverse strengths in your real team?"

### Connecting to Workplace Diversity

**Key Message:**
Just as each ability made players approach the game differently, diverse team members bring different strengths. Success comes from:
- **Recognizing** each person's unique contributions
- **Valuing** different approaches equally
- **Coordinating** complementary strengths
- **Adapting** strategy to leverage diversity

**Real Workplace Parallels:**
- **The Connector:** Relationship builders, cross-functional collaborators
- **The Observer:** Data analysts, active listeners, detail-oriented thinkers
- **The Advocate:** Sponsors, allies, those who elevate others
- **The Innovator:** Creative thinkers, disruptors, vision-casters
- **The Mentor:** Experienced guides, knowledge sharers, patient teachers
- **The Adapter:** Cultural bridges, change navigators, flexible problem-solvers
- **The Coordinator:** Project managers, organizers, systems thinkers
- **The Champion:** Change agents, courageous truth-tellers, activists

### Common Insights from Play

**"My ability seemed weak at first but became crucial later"**
→ All strengths are valuable; context determines when

**"I had to think completely differently with my ability"**
→ Diversity means different mental models and approaches

**"We succeeded when we combined abilities strategically"**
→ Synergy comes from coordination, not just aggregation

**"Some abilities helped others more than the player themselves"**
→ True strength often means enabling others' success

## Customization Options

### Creating New Abilities
For repeat play or specific themes, facilitators can create custom abilities:

**Template:**
- **Name:** What is the ability called?
- **Power:** What can the player do?
- **Frequency:** Once per round? Passive? Limited uses?
- **Rules:** Specific constraints and timing
- **Symbolic Of:** What workplace strength does this represent?

**Example Custom Ability: The Translator**
- **Power:** May explain one other player's gesture/signal in words, even during communication restrictions
- **Symbolic Of:** Those who bridge language and cultural gaps
- **Use When:** Playing with international teams

### Renault-Specific Abilities
Work with Renault to create abilities reflecting specific valued strengths in their culture.

---

## Learning Objectives: Player Abilities

By experiencing asymmetrical abilities, players will:

1. **Value Different Contributions:** No single "best" way to contribute
2. **Experience Complementarity:** Different strengths work together
3. **Practice Coordination:** Diverse teams must actively align
4. **Recognize Hidden Strengths:** Undervalued abilities prove crucial
5. **Personal Connection:** Reflect on their own unique workplace strengths

**Most Importantly:** Players experience viscerally that diversity isn't about "tolerance" - it's about **strategic advantage**. The team is stronger *because* everyone plays differently.

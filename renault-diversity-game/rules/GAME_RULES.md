# Renault Diversity & Inclusion: The Cooperative Challenge

## Game Overview

**Players:** 3-6 players
**Duration:** 45-90 minutes (including facilitated discussion)
**Type:** Cooperative trick-taking card game with asymmetrical player abilities

## Objective

Work together as a team to complete as many diversity and inclusion challenges as possible. Each challenge represents real workplace scenarios at Renault. The team wins or loses together, reflecting the collaborative nature of building an inclusive workplace.

## Components

### Playing Cards (Standard Deck)
- 4 suits representing D&I dimensions:
  - **Hearts (Red):** Representation & Hiring
  - **Diamonds (Red):** Career Development & Promotion
  - **Clubs (Black):** Workplace Culture & Belonging
  - **Spades (Black):** Voice & Participation
- Cards numbered 1-13 in each suit
- Total: 52 playing cards

### Challenge Deck
- 20 challenge cards with progressive difficulty
- Each card presents a team objective based on real Renault D&I scenarios
- Challenges modify game rules and create constraints

### Gold Deck
- 10 Gold Cards linking game mechanics to D&I concepts
- Revealed at milestone moments during gameplay
- Provides framework for facilitated discussion

### Player Ability Cards
- 6 unique ability cards (one per player)
- Each player has a different power reflecting diverse strengths
- Abilities impact trick-taking and card management

### Score Track
- Tracks team progress through Challenge Deck
- Marks milestones for Gold Card reveals

## Setup

1. **Choose Difficulty Level:**
   - Beginner: Use Challenges 1-10
   - Advanced: Use Challenges 11-20
   - Expert: Use full deck shuffled randomly

2. **Distribute Player Ability Cards:**
   - Shuffle and deal one ability card to each player face-up
   - Players read and understand their unique ability
   - Briefly discuss how abilities might complement each other

3. **Prepare Challenge Deck:**
   - Arrange Challenge cards in order (or shuffle for expert mode)
   - Place deck face-down where all can see
   - Reveal the first Challenge card

4. **Prepare Gold Deck:**
   - Place Gold Cards face-down in a separate pile
   - These will be revealed at specific milestones

5. **Deal Playing Cards:**
   - Shuffle the 52-card playing deck
   - Deal cards evenly to all players
   - Players may look at their own cards
   - **Communication Rule:** Players CANNOT share specific card details unless Challenge allows it

## How to Play

### Game Flow

The game progresses through multiple rounds, with each round attempting to complete one Challenge card.

### Round Structure

1. **Challenge Phase**
   - Read the current Challenge card aloud
   - Understand the win condition and any rule modifications
   - Limited discussion allowed (see Communication Rules)

2. **Trick-Taking Phase**
   - Play proceeds clockwise from the starting player
   - Each player plays one card to the center (this is a "trick")
   - Continue until all cards in hand are played

3. **Evaluation Phase**
   - Check if Challenge win condition was met
   - If successful: Advance to next Challenge, reveal Gold Card if at milestone
   - If failed: Game ends, calculate final score

### Trick-Taking Rules

**Basic Mechanics:**
1. The starting player plays any card from their hand
2. Other players must **follow suit** if possible (play the same suit)
3. If a player cannot follow suit, they may play any card
4. The player who played the **highest number** of the **led suit** wins the trick
5. The winner of the trick leads the next trick

**Important:**
- You must follow suit if you have a card of the led suit
- If you cannot follow suit, you cannot win the trick (even with a higher number)
- The won tricks are placed face-up in front of the winner

### Communication Rules

**Default Communication:**
- Players may discuss general strategy
- Players may NOT reveal specific cards ("I have the 8 of Hearts")
- Players may share:
  - General hand strength ("I'm strong in red suits")
  - Number of cards in a suit ("I have three Diamonds")
  - Ability to follow suit ("I can follow")

**Challenge Modifications:**
Many Challenges modify communication rules (see Challenge Deck)

### Player Abilities

Each player has a unique ability that can be used during gameplay. Abilities reflect different strengths and perspectives in diverse teams:

- Abilities can typically be used once per round
- Some abilities are passive and always active
- Players should announce when using their ability
- (Specific abilities detailed in Player Ability Cards section)

## Winning and Scoring

### Team Victory Conditions

**Complete Game:**
- Successfully complete all 10 Challenges in your difficulty level
- This is the ultimate victory!

**Partial Success:**
- Score = Number of Challenges completed
- 7-9 Challenges: Strong performance
- 4-6 Challenges: Good progress
- 1-3 Challenges: Learning experience

### Failure Condition

The game ends immediately when the team fails to complete a Challenge's win condition.

## Gold Card Reveals

Gold Cards are revealed at these milestones:
- After Challenge 2
- After Challenge 4
- After Challenge 6
- After Challenge 8
- After Challenge 10
- (Continue every 2 challenges for advanced/expert modes)

When a Gold Card is revealed:
1. Pause gameplay
2. Facilitator reads the Gold Card aloud
3. Team discusses the D&I concept and its connection to the game
4. Team shares examples from their own workplace experience
5. Resume gameplay with next Challenge

## Optional Rules

### Dispersed Rule Learning
Instead of teaching all rules upfront:
1. Divide rules among players (one section each)
2. Each player explains their section to others
3. Learn and clarify rules through play
4. Promotes active engagement and diverse perspectives

### Parallel Team Play
For large groups:
1. Split into teams of 3-6 players
2. All teams play the same Challenge sequence
3. Compare progress and strategies afterward
4. Rich opportunity for discussing different approaches

### Progressive Difficulty
After completing a difficulty level, try:
- Next difficulty level
- Same challenges with reduced communication
- Random Challenge order (expert shuffle)

## Game Design Principles

### Cooperative
- All players win or lose together
- Success requires coordination and trust
- Builds team cohesion through shared challenge

### Asymmetrical
- Each player has different abilities
- No two players approach the game the same way
- Highlights value of diverse perspectives and strengths

### Rules-Light
- Core mechanic (trick-taking) is simple
- Complexity emerges from Challenges
- Focus remains on concepts, not complicated rules

## Tips for Success

1. **Use Your Abilities Wisely:** Don't waste powerful abilities early
2. **Communicate Creatively:** Work within communication constraints
3. **Plan Ahead:** Think about future tricks, not just the current one
4. **Trust Your Team:** Sometimes you need to take risks based on limited information
5. **Learn from Failure:** Each failed Challenge teaches you about team dynamics

## Facilitator Notes

- Allow organic discussion during Gold Card reveals
- Connect game moments to real workplace scenarios
- Encourage personal reflection and commitment to action
- Keep energy positive and learning-focused
- Total session time: 90-120 minutes including debrief

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the game, players will:
1. Experience the value of diverse abilities working together
2. Recognize how communication barriers impact collaboration
3. Understand the challenge of coordinated action with limited information
4. Identify inclusive vs. exclusive behaviors in their own context
5. Commit to at least one personal behavior change
